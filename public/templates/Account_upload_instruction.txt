Account Upload Template - Instructions
======================================

Please follow the instructions below while filling in the account upload template.

Note:
- (M) = Mandatory – must be filled
- (O) = Optional – can be left blank
- Do NOT remove or rename the column headers
- Ensure data format is clean and consistent to avoid upload errors

----------------------------------
FIELD-BY-FIELD INSTRUCTIONS
----------------------------------


1. Account Name (M)
   - Full name of the account or organization
   - Example: XYZ Medicals

2. Address1 (M)
   - Primary address line
   - Example: 123 Main Street

3. Address2 (O)
   - Additional address info (e.g., Suite 101, Floor 2)

4. Zip (M)
   - 5-digit postal code
   - Example: 10001

5. City (M)
   - Name of the city
   - Example: New York

6. State (M)
   - Full name or abbreviation of the state
   - Example: NY or New York

7. Email (M)
   - Valid email format
   - Example: contact@xyzmedicals.com

8. Phone (M)
   - Enter 10-digit phone number (excluding country code)
   - Example: 2125551234

9. Fax (O)
    - Optional fax number

-------------------------------
✅ SAMPLE DATA ROW:
-------------------------------

 Account Name | Address1      | Address2 | Zip   | City      | State | Email                  | Phone      | Fax       |
--------------|---------------|----------|-------|-----------|-------|------------------------|------------|-----------|
 XYZ Medicals | 123 Main St   | Suite 101| 10001 | New York  | NY    | contact@xyz.com        | 2125551234 | 2125554321 |

-------------------------------
IMPORTANT REMINDERS:
-------------------------------
- Do not leave any mandatory (M) fields empty
- Email should be in correct format (e.g., name@domain.com)
- Phone must be numeric and without special characters
- Do not include the country code in the phone field

For issues or support, please contact your system administrator.
